# Hushlings Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Hushlings is a card game for iPhone and iPad, published by J. van Rekum ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens with your information when you play.

## The short version

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Hushlings has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

## Information kept on your device

The game saves the following on your own device so that it is still there the next time you play:

- your coins, night lights and card backs;
- your settings for sound, music, vibration and difficulty;
- your statistics and album;
- the date of your last daily gift.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app.

## Online play through Game Center

Playing online is optional and uses Apple's Game Center. When you play an online game, Apple connects you to another player, and the two devices exchange the moves of that game. Your opponent sees your Game Center nickname. We do not receive or store your nickname, your Game Center account or the games you play.

Apple handles Game Center under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple whether a purchase succeeded, so that it can add your coins or unlock your theme.

## Children

Hushlings does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. In-app purchases can be restricted with Screen Time on the device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
