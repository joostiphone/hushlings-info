# Hushlings Support

Hushlings is a cozy card game for iPhone and iPad.

## Get in touch

Questions, problems or ideas? Send an email to **hello@2secret.app** and mention "Hushlings" in the subject. We usually reply within a few days.

It helps if you include:

- your device (for example iPhone 16 or iPad Air) and iOS version;
- what you were doing when the problem happened;
- a screenshot, if you have one.

## Common questions

**I hear no sound.**
Open Settings in the game (the gear on the start screen, or the menu during a game) and check that Sound effects and Music are switched on. Also check the volume buttons on your device.

**My purchase did not arrive.**
Open the Shop with the coin button and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom. Coin packs are added as soon as Apple confirms the payment; this can take a moment on a slow connection.

**How do I play online?**
Tap "Play online" on the start screen. You need to be signed in to Game Center in the Settings app of your device.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com
